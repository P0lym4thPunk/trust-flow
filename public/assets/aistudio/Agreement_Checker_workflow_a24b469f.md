Agreement Checker workflow
Check:
- Deliverables are specific enough to understand.
- Completion condition is observable and unambiguous.
- Payer, recipient and approver roles are clear.
- Amount and asset are present and valid.
- Deadlines and time zones are unambiguous where relevant.
- Cancellation/refund expectations are stated.
- Approval thresholds are coherent.
- Terms do not contradict one another.
- The agreement does not promise a capability the contract does not implement.
Return structured findings:
- severity: BLOCKING, WARNING, SUGGESTION
- field
- issue
- why_it_matters
- suggested_revision
The checker does not certify legal validity, fairness, identity, delivery or evidence truth.
